You Found a Performing Artist's "Explainer Page"

This page was written by her manager, Michael Widener II.
The artist's performing name is, "Aeleth."
Her real name is, Natalee Trevino.

Welcome to the official "explainer page" for Aeleth — a landing page and introduction page combined. She's our newest recording artist, and her voice is simply amazing.

We discovered Aeleth while she was taking care of others in her day job, and to our amazement, she had already won over the entire office. There were days when I was in the office and genuinely could not tell the difference between her voice and whatever was playing through the office ceiling speakers.

Then one day, the staff started joking around with the sound system — turning the music down, then back up — until Miss Aeleth finally agreed to sing an entire song for them with no radio, no background music, and the office door closed so I could hear what everyone else already knew.

I heard it!

I honestly thought I had been imagining that voice. I kept thinking the office had some hidden Lucasfilm THX sound processor tucked away in a closet.

It didn't.

It was her.

And she agreed — happily, in fact — to become our very first recording artist.

Head over to our page and hear what happens when talent meets opportunity. You will not hear a full high-definition version because that would make it easy to download and misuse. Instead, you'll hear a "down-rez'ed" version.

("Rez" is short for "resolution," a technical term referring to the quality of a digital item — whether image or audio.)

But you'll still get the picture anyway. We promise.
